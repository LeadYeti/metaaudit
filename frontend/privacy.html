Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

Lead Yeti Pro ("we", "us", "our") operates the Meta Ads Audit tool at audit.leadyetipro.com. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

When you use our tool, we access the following through your Facebook login:

  • Your name and email address — to identify you and follow up about your audit results
  • Your ad account data — campaign structure, ad sets, ads, and performance metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, etc.) to generate your audit report

How We Use Your Data

  • Generate your free Meta Ads audit report in real time
  • Send your contact information to our CRM so we can follow up with audit insights
  • Improve our audit analysis over time

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not store your ad account data. It is fetched, analyzed, and displayed in real time. Nothing is saved to a database.
  • We do not post, edit, or modify anything on your Facebook account or ad account.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share your ad account data with anyone.

Facebook Permissions

We request the following Facebook permissions:

  • email — to capture your email for follow-up
  • public_profile — to get your name
  • ads_read — to read your campaign, ad set, and ad data (read-only)
  • read_insights — to read performance metrics like spend, impressions, and CPA
  • business_management — to list the ad accounts you have access to

All access is read-only. We cannot make changes to your ads or account.

Data Retention

Your ad data is not stored. Your name and email are stored in our CRM for follow-up purposes. You can request deletion of your contact information at any time by emailing us.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services:

  • Facebook/Meta API — to access your ad data with your permission
  • GoHighLevel (GHL) — our CRM where your contact information is stored
  • Railway — our hosting provider
